# Bailleurs, écoles et paiement de dépenses planifiées (loyer, caution, terrain, frais scolaires) — Cameroun et comparables africains

Scope: facts for MoloPay model C ("planned expense": save toward rent/deposit/school fees, platform pays verified landlord/school on client confirmation; lease on file for rent; title verified for land). Research date: 20 Nov 2026. Labels used: **[VÉRIFIÉ]** = stated by a press/official/institutional source fetched or quoted; **[ALLÉGATION]** = commercial/marketing or single unverified source; **[NON VÉRIFIÉ]** = researcher's background knowledge, not confirmed in this session.

## 1. Rental practices in Cameroon (advance rent, deposit, rents, démarcheurs, legal framework)

### Takeaway
The dominant pain point is the lump sum at move-in: landlords in Douala/Yaoundé commonly demand 6–12 months of advance rent plus ~2 months' deposit, with no effective enforced cap; démarcheurs take one month's rent from each side plus visit fees. This lump-sum structure is exactly what a "save then pay the landlord" product targets, but the legal framework on advance rent is thin and poorly documented online.

### Cited Findings
- **Advance rent levels.** In Yaoundé student housing, landlords "imposent dès lors des avances de 10 voire 12 mois en versement unique, au lieu d'un mois de loyer"; the deposit demanded exceeded two months' rent, which the article calls contrary to regulations in force (text not named); rents were 2–3× the approved ("homologués") student-housing grid; the Minister of Higher Education (Jacques Fame Ndongo) convened Commerce and Habitat (MINHDU) ministries, landlords and students and ordered respect of approved rates (10 Sept 2021) **[VÉRIFIÉ]** — [Cameroon Tribune, 10/09/2021](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/42289/en.html/details_2)
- In Douala, 6 months or even one year of rent in advance is demanded of young workers (2018) **[VÉRIFIÉ, search snippet; page returned 406]** — [Africanews, 26/06/2018](https://www.africanews.com/2018/06/26/cameroon-high-rent-advance-crippling-young-working-youths//)
- Neighbourhood breakdown (deposit + advance, long-term unfurnished): Bonapriso/Bastos 2 + 10–12 months; Akwa/Bonanjo/centre-ville 2 + 8–10; Makepe/Logpom/Biyem-Assi 2 + 6–8; popular neighbourhoods 2 + 6 (sometimes less with private landlords). Example: rent 500,000 FCFA in Bonapriso → 6–7 M FCFA due before keys; overall 4–7 M FCFA range. Refunds of deposit "typically" 30–60 days; recommends traceable payment (mobile money/transfer), refundable-deposit receipt, photographed inventory **[ALLÉGATION — marketing blog, unsourced, 28/04/2026]** — [KasaStay](https://www.kasastay.com/fr/blog/caution-location-cameroun)
- Fiscal simulator states 2 months' deposit is most common in Douala/Yaoundé (3 for commercial premises) and some landlords demand 14 months (12 advance + 2 deposit); distinguishes caution (refundable guarantee) from avance (prepayment of future rent, 3–14 months) **[ALLÉGATION — commercial tool]** — [LeFisk simulateur loyer](https://lefisk.cm/outils/sim-loyer)
- Commercial example: a Yaoundé centre-ville office listing asks 12 months' advance + 800,000 FCFA deposit **[VÉRIFIÉ as listing, search snippet]** — [Expat.com listing](https://www.expat.com/fr/immobilier/afrique/cameroun/yaounde/77-locaux-commerciaux-a-louer/815075-plusieurs-espaces-commerciaux-a-louer-avec-ascenseur-au-centre-ville.html)
- **Rent inflation / no regulation.** Mid-range apartment rents in Yaoundé "ont doublé en moins de trois ans"; each promoter sets its own rent and number of deposit months; article cites a "vide juridique" and "absence de régulation stricte"; no official quoted, no figures by neighbourhood (14/08/2025) **[VÉRIFIÉ as press claim]** — [Camer.be / AllAfrica, 14/08/2025](https://www.camer.be/amp/90242/12:1/cameroun-explosion-des-loyers-a-yaounde-la-flambee-immobiliere-qui-inquiete-habitants-et-experts-cameroon.html)
- **Démarcheurs.** Each lease earns the démarcheur a double commission — one month's rent from the landlord and one from the tenant (sometimes negotiated down); prospects also pay 5,000–10,000 FCFA per visit; démarcheurs dominate the market despite mistrust; article mentions unspecified "recent reforms" to regulate the real-estate agent profession (Forbes Afrique, 6 May 2024, Patrick Nelle; quotes Douala démarcheur "Vincent" and Hortense Leconte, director of Lion's Immo, Douala) **[VÉRIFIÉ]** — [Forbes Afrique, 06/05/2024](https://forbesafrique.com/cameroun-vers-la-fin-des-demarcheurs/)
- **Formality of housing.** >50% of Cameroon's housing stock considered informal; >90% of construction self-built via informal sector; cheapest SIC (state company) rental unit 50,000 FCFA/month (World Bank, April 2020) **[VÉRIFIÉ]** — [World Bank, Le financement du logement au Cameroun, 2020](http://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/358971591000376962/txt/Financement-du-Logement-au-Cameroun-Vers-un-Logement-Abordable-pour-Tous.txt)
- **Tenant-side sanctions.** Penal Code art. 322-1 ("filouterie de loyer") punishes a tenant under a duly registered lease who owes two months' rent and neither pays nor vacates one month after formal notice; SIC (under MINHDU) ran a campaign against filouteries and sublets **[VÉRIFIÉ, press]** — [Camer.be](https://www.camer.be/amp/53774/11:1/cameroun-filouteries-de-loyer-sale-temps-pour-les-locataires-vereux-cameroon.html); [Ecomatin](https://ecomatin.net/logements-sic-campagne-contre-les-filouteries-de-loyer-et-les-sous-locations/)
- **Legal cap — conflicting claims.** KasaStay asserts "la loi camerounaise plafonne la caution à 3 mois de loyer maximum" without citing a text **[ALLÉGATION]** — [KasaStay](https://www.kasastay.com/fr/blog/caution-location-cameroun); Cameroon Tribune (2021) implies a 2-month deposit limit in "regulations in force" **[VÉRIFIÉ as press claim, text unnamed]** — [Cameroon Tribune](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/42289/en.html/details_2); a rental blog cites "loi n° 84-01 du 4 novembre 1984" for residential leases **[ALLÉGATION, not checked against official text]** — [appartementmeubleyaounde.com](https://appartementmeubleyaounde.com/securiser-son-bail-a-yaounde-evitez-les-erreurs-fatales-des-nouveaux-arrivants-analyse-approfondie-n9/)
- Disputes: large institutional rent disputes also exist (e.g., SCI Bonanjo suing Communauté Urbaine de Douala over 14 months of unpaid rent, 2025) — shows landlord-side fear of non-payment — [Camer.be](https://www.camer.be/amp/87798/11:1/cameroun-la-cud-traduite-en-justice-pour-une-affaire-14-mois-de-loyers-impayes-cameroon.html)

### Inferences
- Model C's natural product: a "pack d'entrée" goal = deposit (≈2 months) + advance (6–12 months) + démarcheur commission (1 month) ≈ 9–15 months of rent. For a 100,000 FCFA/month flat that is ~0.9–1.5 M FCFA — a 6–12 month savings horizon. The commission line matters because tenants pay it in cash at signature.
- Since the platform pays the landlord on confirmation, it should split the payout: deposit (refundable, ideally tracked as such with its own receipt), advance (tied to lease dates), agent fee (separate beneficiary). This mirrors the caution/avance distinction landlords and tenants already use.
- Because no clearly enforceable cap is documented, MoloPay should not refuse payouts exceeding 2–3 months' deposit, but could display a neutral notice ("la réglementation évoquée limite la caution à 2–3 mois") only after legal confirmation.
- A "lease on file" requirement is realistic only if MoloPay accepts informal written leases/receipts (photo of signed bail, landlord ID, receipt), given >50% informal housing; requiring a registered lease (enregistrement fiscal) would exclude most landlords. Note however that art. 322-1 protection for landlords applies to *registered* leases — a selling point MoloPay could offer landlords (help registering).
- Démarcheurs are both a risk (fraud, fake landlords) and a distribution channel (they meet every tenant); a referral/agent role with verified ID could turn them into allies.

### Gaps
- Exact legal text governing residential leases in Cameroon not confirmed online: **[NON VÉRIFIÉ]** background knowledge is that residential leases fall under the Code civil (arts. 1708 ff., French civil code as applicable in francophone Cameroon) and common law in anglophone regions; OHADA's Acte uniforme sur le droit commercial général (revised 2010) covers only professional/commercial leases, not habitation; loi n° 97/003 du 10 janvier 1997 concerns *promotion immobilière* (developers), not rents. Need lawyer/primary text confirmation, plus whether any decree caps advance rent (none found).
- No 2025–2026 MINHDU statement or circular on abusive advance rent was found.
- No reliable survey of rents by neighbourhood (FCFA) from press or statistics (INS) was found; only marketing figures.
- Share of renters in Douala/Yaoundé not found in the World Bank document's first half (second half unread).
- No data found on how landlords currently receive rent (cash vs MoMo) — likely cash with handwritten receipts, but unsourced.

## 2. Land/real-estate fraud and title verification (titre foncier, MINDCAF)

### Takeaway
Double sales and fraudulent/duplicate titles are a recurring, well-documented problem in Douala and Yaoundé, and no public online title-verification service was found; verification today means physical checks at the Conservation foncière / cadastre, ideally via notary. Model C's "title verified" requirement therefore implies a manual, partner-based verification workflow, not an API.

### Cited Findings
- >70% of buildings in Cameroon lack a formal title; titling involves 5 procedures and costs close to 20% of final property price; processing time fell from 10 years to 6 months, or 81 days in 2019; in 2014 only 13.6% of owner households held a title nationally (Douala 17.4%, Yaoundé 22.8%) **[VÉRIFIÉ]** — [World Bank 2020](http://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/358971591000376962/txt/Financement-du-Logement-au-Cameroun-Vers-un-Logement-Abordable-pour-Tous.txt)
- In the Mbankolo (Yaoundé) disaster case, MINDCAF identified two fraudulent land titles; 1976 decree (27 April 1976, conditions d'obtention du titre foncier) is the basis for withdrawing irregular titles (2023) **[VÉRIFIÉ, press]** — [Ecomatin](https://ecomatin.net/drame-de-mbankolo-le-mindcaf-identifie-deux-titres-fonciers-frauduleux)
- A lawyer quoted: "si aujourd'hui il y a deux titres fonciers, automatiquement un est faux ; l'autre n'a pas été mis à jour au niveau du cadastre"; in a double-sale case an opposition blocks the technical file until lifted **[VÉRIFIÉ, press]** — [Camer.be, double vente](https://www.camer.be/amp/47703/33:30/cameroon-flash-back-sur-le-phenomene-de-la-double-vente-d39un-terrain-que-dit-le-droit-domanial-et-foncier-au-cameroun.html)
- A family obtained cancellation of 5 land titles issued on its property — [Camer.be](https://www.camer.be/amp/76747/11:1/cameroun-une-famille-fait-annuler-5-titres-fonciers-etablis-sur-sa-propriete-cameroon.html); court annulled TF n°2562/Mefou-et-Afamba and all subdivisions (Oct 2024) — [AllAfrica, 08/10/2024](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202410080311.html)
- May 2026: suspicions of land predation, influence peddling and organised forgery threatening Douala and Yaoundé — [AllAfrica, 13/05/2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202605130384.html); June 2026 headline quoting MINDCAF minister Henri Eyebe Ayissi: "L'époque des titres sans vérification est révolue" (content not fetched) — [AllAfrica, 22/06/2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202606220784.html)
- 2025: MINDCAF planned a special unit to re-read, verify and finalise title withdrawal/nullity/rehabilitation projects — [Cameroon Tribune](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/69128/fr.html/details_2); digitisation of the land system and a national parcel database have been announced since ~2016 — [Cameroon Tribune](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/articles/11848/fr/details_2); [Digital Business Africa](https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-une-data-base-pour-securiser-le-titre-foncier/)
- A certificate of possession of customary land rights (CPDFC) was instituted (2024) — an intermediate document, not a title — [Droit Médias Finance](https://droitmediasfinance.com/index.php/actualites/droit-ohada-affaires/717-cameroun-un-certificat-de-possession-des-droits-fonciers-coutumiers-administrativement-reconnus-cpdfc-institue)
- "Le titre foncier est le seul document reconnu légalement — la lettre d'attribution n'est pas un titre foncier"; MINDCAF fees payable online via treasury portal **[ALLÉGATION, commercial site]** — [LeFisk](https://lefisk.cm/blog/recettes-non-fiscales-mindcaf-cameroun)

### Inferences
- "Title verified" should mean: certified copy / *certificat de propriété* obtained from the Conservation foncière of the division, plus cadastral check of the parcel, plus seller ID matching the registered owner, ideally performed by a partner notary or geometer — with the payout made to a notary escrow/account rather than directly to a seller. A self-uploaded photo of a titre foncier is worthless as proof given documented forgeries.
- Since only ~14–23% of owners hold titles, the land feature will serve a narrow segment; MoloPay should clearly refuse "lettre d'attribution"/customary-only sales or label them high-risk.
- Duplicate-title risk also affects *landlords* in rent model: landlord identity verification (is the payee really the owner or mandated agent?) is a fraud control for rent too.

### Gaps
- No public MINDCAF online title-verification tool found; fee and lead time for a *certificat de propriété* not found.
- No quantitative data on land fraud volume (cases/year).

## 3. School fees in Cameroon (levels, calendar, payment methods, school concerns)

### Takeaway
Public secondary "contributions exigibles" and exam fees are already paid digitally by mobile money keyed on a unique pupil matricule from cartescolaire.cm; private schools charge from ~240,000 FCFA (confessional) to millions (international), usually in 3–4 installments. The existing matricule infrastructure is the obvious pupil identifier for MoloPay's school payouts.

### Cited Findings
- 2018: MINESEC signed agreements with mobile payment operators (Orange, MTN, Express Union, Campost) for fee payment by mobile money, aimed at transparency and traceability **[VÉRIFIÉ]** — [Afrik.com](https://www.afrik.com/cameroun-mobile-money-un-nouveau-mode-de-paiement-des-frais-de-scolarite-et-des-examens-officiels)
- 2025/2026: payment of contributions exigibles and MINESEC exam fees opened 3 Sept 2025 in all 10 regions, electronically via MTN Cameroon, Orange Cameroun, Express Union, Campost and Afriland First Bank; pupils identified by unique matricule generated on cartescolaire.cm after school registers them (establishment code used for ENIEG/ENIET exams) **[VÉRIFIÉ]** — [Journal du Cameroun, 03/09/2025](https://fr.journalducameroun.com/cameroun-annee-scolaire-2025-2026-debut-du-paiement-des-contributions-exigibles-et-frais-de-dexamens/)
- Orange Cameroun and Ecobank joined the shortlist of approved operators for digital exam/school fee payment — [Ecomatin](https://ecomatin.net/paiement-numerique-des-frais-dexamens-et-de-scolarite-orange-cameroun-n-et-ecobank-rejoignent-la-shortlist-des-operateurs-agrees)
- Private schools required to register pupils on cartescolaire.cm (deadline 15 Dec 2024 for that year) — search-result summary of [Journal du Cameroun](https://fr.journalducameroun.com/?p=440884) **[VÉRIFIÉ via snippet only]**
- Third-party apps: "MinesecPay" presented as ministry-approved, MTN MoMo online payment fees 200 FCFA (1–10,000) up to 500 FCFA (>30,000) **[ALLÉGATION, blog; fees may be outdated]** — [Temovision](https://temovision.com/fr/pay-school-fees-via-momo-and-minesecpay/); Eska app (Canadian-based developer) claims free school payments with notification to school and SEDUC recommendation **[ALLÉGATION, sponsored]** — [Camer.be](https://www.camer.be/86233/11:1/cameroun-lapplication-mobile-eska-votre-solution-de-paiements-scolaires-la-plus-avancee-et-gratuite-cameroon.html)
- Fee levels: a Yaoundé Catholic school charges externes 240,000 FCFA (1er cycle) / 250,000 FCFA (2nd cycle) incl. 30,000 pre-registration and 4,000 APE, payable in 3 installments within set deadlines (undated) — [Ecomatin](https://ecomatin.net/enseignements-primaires-et-secondaire-catholiques-comment-ils-nous-volent/); British Isles International School Douala 500,000 (maternelle) / 700,000 (primaire) / 1,180,000 FCFA (secondaire); American School of Douala USD 5,000–18,000 **[ALLÉGATION, aggregator]** — [Temovision](https://temovision.com/fr/ecoles-internationales-a-douala/); [ASD](https://asddouala.com/?p=1832)
- Most Yaoundé private schools collect in 3–4 installments **[ALLÉGATION, school-software vendor]** — [Kolonell](https://kolonell.com/fr/blog/logiciel-gestion-ecole-privee-yaounde-prix-fcfa-2026)
- Minister of Secondary Education Pauline Nalova Lyonga: "l'école ne doit pas chasser un enfant parce qu'il n'a pas payé l'APE" — [Ecomatin](https://ecomatin.net/lecole-ne-doit-pas-chasser-un-enfant-parce-quil-na-pas-paye-lape-pauline-nalova-lyonga); Nov 2026: parents in Yaoundé/Douala denounce overbilling of CEP/6e entrance exam fees; basic education minister recalled services to order on 9 Nov 2026 — [Koaci, 10/11/2026](https://www.koaci.com/article/2026/11/10/cameroun/politique/cameroun-frais-dexamens-les-parents-denoncent-une-arnaque-a-ciel-ouvert-dans-les-ecoles-primaires_202466.html)

### Inferences
- Calendar: rentrée early September → savings goals should target a 1st installment due at/just before rentrée, with 2nd/3rd tranches (commonly ~Nov–Jan and ~Feb–Mar — exact dates unverified) as separate dated sub-goals. Peak load late Aug–Sept.
- Pupil identifier: request the cartescolaire matricule (secondary) plus school's establishment code and class; for primary/maternelle (no matricule) use name + class + school-issued reference.
- Parents' trust issue is overbilling/opaque extra fees; a platform that pays only against a school's published fee schedule and issues an itemised receipt addresses that pain.

### Gaps
- No official 2025–2026 fee grid for private schools; no data on share of private schools accepting MoMo merchant payments or using bank accounts; no Cameroonian school-fee savings/loan startup with traction data found.
- Private school reconciliation complaints in Cameroon not documented in sources found (inferred from Kenya/Uganda).

## 4. Comparable platforms elsewhere (rent saving/financing, school-fee payments)

### Takeaway
Comparable African players show (a) landlords strongly prefer lump-sum annual payment and resist monthly schemes, so the platform must pay landlords upfront/in full; (b) school payment platforms win by giving each pupil a unique payment reference that auto-reconciles across all channels, with large scale reached in Uganda (SchoolPay).

### Cited Findings
- **Nigeria — landlords.** Landlords are the main obstacle; many prefer annual upfront rent because monthly payment seems unsustainable and annual collection reduces admin cost and default risk — TechCabal 2021 (search summary; page 403) — [TechCabal, 19/01/2021](https://techcabal.com/2021/01/19/nigerian-property-tech-market/)
- **Kwaba** (founded 2019, Lagos/Abuja): "Rent-Now-Pay-Later" finances annual rent upfront, tenant repays monthly over 6–12 months; firm cites ~6% monthly interest **[ALLÉGATION, as reported]** — [Nairametrics, 2021](https://nairametrics.com/2021/05/19/real-estate-firms-that-allow-you-to-pay-rent-as-you-earn); [VC4A](https://vc4a.com/ventures/kwaba)
- **Spleet**: started as monthly-rent marketplace, pivoted to embedded finance because the model couldn't scale; $2.6M seed (Oct 2022, MaC VC); no-collateral rent loans up to ₦3M at ~3.5%/month; NPL < 2% in beta; landlords can still receive rent yearly — [TechCabal, 04/10/2022](https://techcabal.com/2022/10/04/spleet-raises-2-6-million-seed-round-to-provide-rent-financing-across-africa/); [TechCabal, 16/03/2022](https://techcabal.com/2022/03/16/how-spleet-became-a-fintech-solution/)
- **Rent Small Small / SmallSmall**: monthly rent for tenants, tenant vetting for landlords; raised $3M (Oct 2022) — [TechCrunch, 31/10/2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/31/nigerian-proptech-smallsmall-raises-3m-to-provide-flexible-living-solutions-for-customers); [TechCabal, 20/04/2022](https://techcabal.com/2022/04/20/how-rentsmallsmall-is-expanding-the-frontiers-in-real-estate/)
- **Uganda — SchoolPay**: started 2016 with one school; ~11,000 schools and >6 million students by 2023 — [AllAfrica, 29/09/2023](https://allafrica.com/stories/202309290680.html); >15,000 schools and over half of private-sector tuition payments (Stanbic executive, date unclear) — [The Independent UG](https://www.independent.co.ug/financing-educations-next-frontier); >UGX 3 trillion/year transactions (officials) — [Nile Post](https://nilepost.co.ug/news/322713/schoolpay-npeia-enter-partnership-to-drive-digital-transformation-in-ugandas-private-education-sector?amp=1); MTN MoMo–SchoolPay 10-year partnership marked Aug 2026 — [TechAfricaNews, 31/08/2026](https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/31/mtn-momo-schoolpay-10-year-digital-school-fees-partnership/); schools see all payments from any channel in real time (UNCDF 2021) — [UNCDF](https://www.uncdf.org/article/6666/uncdf-partners-with-service-cops-to-scale-up-digital-education-technologies-in-uganda); UNCDF/SIDA grant to scale SchoolPay in rural Northern Uganda, where schools relied on cash and paper (2023) — [UNCDF, 2023](https://www.uncdf.org/article/8166/extending-the-frontiers-of-digital-school-fees-payments). Company figures are partner/company claims, not audited.
- Uganda direct-to-school digital loans reportedly helped 31,000 learners avoid dropout (May 2026) — [AllAfrica, 15/05/2026](https://allafrica.com/stories/202605150189.html); Stanbic + Fincom digital lending for schools (Nov 2025) — [AllAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202511270589.html)
- **Kenya**: parents pay to school's M-Pesa paybill with the pupil's admission number as account reference; Safaricom "Lipa Karo" — [Safaricom FAQ](https://safaricom.co.ke/media-center-landing/frequently-asked-questions/lipa-karo); M-Pesa Bill Manager offers invoicing, e-receipts and real-time reconciliation — [Safaricom PDF](https://www.safaricom.co.ke/images/Downloads/m-pesa-bill-manager-for-schools.pdf); KCB Lipa Karo needs only student name + admission number, tagged for reconciliation — [KCB](https://ke.kcbgroup.com/how-to-pay-school-fees-instantly-via-kcb-mobile-app-back-to-school-2026); parents frequently enter wrong admission numbers, requiring reversals — vendor guide [paybillke](https://paybillke.com/guides/mpesa-for-schools-2026) **[ALLÉGATION]**; CGAP deck: ~4,000 schools accepting M-Pesa (date unclear) — [CGAP](https://cgap.org/sites/default/files/publications/slidedeck/cgapdigitalfinanceeducationworkshopreport-160712132204.pdf)
- Kenya school-fee finance/savings: Aspira Soma loan up to KSh 500,000 (2023) and KSh 23M lent in its first year — [Capital FM, 08/2023](https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/business/2023/08/parents-to-access-up-to-sh500000-aspira-school-fees-loan-online/), [Capital FM, 12/2023](https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/business/2023/12/kenyans-borrow-sh23-million-from-aspiras-soma-loan-facility-to-pay-school-fees); KaroPay/M-Soma savings from KSh 10 toward school fees — [FSD Africa, 2023](https://fsdafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/FSD-Africa-Impact-BimaLab-Kenya-KaroPay-09.11.2023-2.pdf); Jackfruit Finance short-term loans to low-cost Nairobi schools — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/research-and-studies/keeping-kids-in-school-how-fintech-is-helping-parents-in-nairobis-densest-slum-myggtcaj); parents increasingly turn to loan apps — [Standard](https://standardmedia.co.ke/business/article/2001489317/cash-strapped-parents-now-turn-to-loan-apps-for-school-fees)

### Inferences
- MoloPay model C (save-first, no credit) avoids the default risk that drove Kwaba/Spleet pricing (3.5–6%/month) and matches landlords' preference: landlord receives the full lump sum once. Positioning for landlords: "loyer annuel garanti, payé en une fois, traçable".
- SchoolPay/Lipa Karo show that the key to school adoption is a per-pupil reference that reconciles automatically, a school dashboard showing all payments in real time, and a mobile-money operator partnership; MoloPay's school side should replicate this (matricule as reference, CSV export).
- Monthly-rent marketplace models struggled to scale in Nigeria (Spleet pivot) — evidence against pushing landlords to accept monthly rent via MoloPay.

### Gaps
- SchoolPay's per-transaction fee to parents and payout timing to schools not found (pages blocked). No Ghana school-fee platform data collected. Current status (2025–2026) of Kwaba/Spleet/SmallSmall not verified.

## 5. Beneficiary (landlord/school) and payer UX expectations

### Takeaway
From the evidence, beneficiaries need: unambiguous payer/pupil identification, itemised receipts, real-time notification, reconciliation exports, and payout to the channel they already use (MoMo for informal landlords, bank/MoMo merchant for schools); payers need traceable proof (deposit vs advance), protection against fake landlords/titles, and installment-aligned goals.

### Cited Findings
- Pupil identification via unique reference is standard (matricule in Cameroon; admission number in Kenya; payment code in Uganda) — [Journal du Cameroun 2025](https://fr.journalducameroun.com/cameroun-annee-scolaire-2025-2026-debut-du-paiement-des-contributions-exigibles-et-frais-de-dexamens/); [KCB](https://ke.kcbgroup.com/how-to-pay-school-fees-instantly-via-kcb-mobile-app-back-to-school-2026); [UNCDF](https://www.uncdf.org/article/6666/uncdf-partners-with-service-cops-to-scale-up-digital-education-technologies-in-uganda)
- Mis-keyed references are a common reconciliation failure; search by transaction ID and reversal procedures needed **[ALLÉGATION]** — [paybillke](https://paybillke.com/guides/mpesa-for-schools-2026)

### Inferences
- Landlord KYC should be tiered: tier 1 (CNI + MoMo number in same name + signed lease/receipt photo + geolocated address) for small payouts; tier 2 (proof of ownership: titre foncier copy or *mandat de gestion* for agents) above a threshold. Heavy KYC (registered company, bank account) would exclude most informal landlords.
- Payout default: MoMo to landlord's name-matched wallet (name-match check against operator KYC), optional bank transfer for agencies/schools. Avoid paying démarcheurs on landlord's behalf without a written mandate.
- Confirmation flow: tenant confirms "j'ai visité / bail signé / clés remises" → landlord confirms amount and period → payout → auto-generated quittance distinguishing caution/avance/commission.
- School UX: school onboarding with fee schedule per class and tranche; parent selects school + pupil matricule; school dashboard + Excel/CSV export by class/tranche; SMS/WhatsApp notification to school bursar and parent.

### Gaps
- No Cameroon-specific survey of landlord willingness to use a third-party platform, or of school bursars' reconciliation practices, was found; primary research (interviews with ~10 landlords/agents and ~5 school bursars in Douala/Yaoundé) recommended.
- Regulatory question of whether holding savings and paying third parties requires COBAC/BEAC e-money or payment-institution licensing was out of scope here.
