# Economics and risks of a "frozen price" (price lock) promise for MoloPay (Cameroon)

Scope: merchant/platform exposure when the price of a product (phone, motorbike, fridge, building materials, rent) is fixed at reservation and held for 12–24 months while the customer saves. Period 2020–2026. Research date: 27 Nov 2026. Estimates are labelled **[ESTIMATE]**; anything I could not source is in the Gaps sections.

---

## 1. Inflation and price dynamics in Cameroon/CEMAC, 2020–2026

### Takeaway
Cameroon went through a sharp inflation shock in 2022–2024: 6.3%, then 7.4%, then 4.5%, for cumulative CPI growth of 19.3% over three years. Inflation eased to about 3.4% in 2025 and about 2.6% on average through August 2026. The general price level drift is therefore now low single digits a year. The real risk sits in specific categories: transport/fuel (+15% in 2023, +12.3% in 2024), building materials (cement up about 25–30% in 2024–25), imported food, and administered or tax-driven step changes such as the fuel price hikes and phone customs enforcement. A 12–24 month lock exposes merchants to roughly 3–9% generic drift plus category step-shocks of 15–30%.

### Cited Findings
**Headline CPI (INS / IMF)**
- 2022 inflation: 6.3% — [INS monthly brief Dec 2024](https://ins-cameroun.cm/en/statistique/monthly-note-on-the-evolution-of-household-final-consumer-prices-in-cameroon-december-2024/)
- 2023 inflation: 7.4%; 2024 inflation: 4.5% (down 2.9 points). Cumulative inflation over 2022–2024 was 19.3%, against 17.5% over the nine years 2013–2021 — [INS, Monitoring inflation in Cameroon: 2024 and outlook 2025 (PDF)](https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Annual-Report-on-Price-Trends-in-Cameroon-in-2024-1.pdf)
- Caution: the "6.8% / 5.9% / 4.2%" figures sometimes shown for 2023/2024/2025 are IMF **global** inflation numbers quoted inside the INS note, not Cameroon figures — [same INS PDF](https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Annual-Report-on-Price-Trends-in-Cameroon-in-2024-1.pdf)
- 2024 breakdown: food +5.6% (11.1% in 2023); transport +12.3% (+15% in 2023); energy +7.8%; fresh produce +9.2%; core inflation +3.1%. Food and transport make up about 44% of household spending — [INS 2024 annual note](https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Annual-Report-on-Price-Trends-in-Cameroon-in-2024-1.pdf)
- 2024 by origin: local products +4.7%, **imported products +4.0%** — [INS 2024 annual note](https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Annual-Report-on-Price-Trends-in-Cameroon-in-2024-1.pdf)
- 2024 by city: Douala 5.1% (among the highest); range from 3.0% in Garoua to 7.0% in Maroua — [INS 2024 annual note](https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Annual-Report-on-Price-Trends-in-Cameroon-in-2024-1.pdf)
- Year-on-year inflation was 5.0% in December 2024 and 5.3% in January 2025; core inflation 3.0% — [INS monthly notes, search summary](https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Note-sur-les-prix-au-Cameroun-en-janvier-2025.pdf)
- 2025 average inflation was about 3.4%, driven by easing food and transport prices; the IMF projected 2.9% for 2026 — [IMF 2026 Article IV conclusion (Mar 2026)](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/03/30/pr-26096-cameroon-imf-executive-board-concludes-2026-article-iv-consultation) (the 3.4% figure comes via the search summary of IMF/related material; confirm in the [staff report](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/cr/issues/2026/04/03/cameroon-2026-article-iv-consultation-press-release-staff-report-and-statement-by-the-575214))
- Average inflation through August 2026 was 2.6%, still declining, but the IMF expects the trend to reverse on accelerating food price pressure. An international oil price shock is expected to generate "sizeable fuel subsidies" in 2026. GDP growth was 3.5% in both 2024 and 2025 — [IMF staff statement, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/10/01/pr26314-imf-staff-concludes-assesment-cameroon)
- CEMAC: the BEAC forecast 4.2% inflation for 2024, down from 5.6% in 2023, against a community norm of 3% — [cited in INS 2024 note](https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Annual-Report-on-Price-Trends-in-Cameroon-in-2024-1.pdf)

**Exchange rate (fixed peg 655.957 FCFA = 1 EUR)**
- INS lists the depreciation of the CFA franc against the USD as an inflation factor for imports and industrial inputs. One USD averaged **625.97 FCFA in December 2024** — [INS 2024 note](https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Annual-Report-on-Price-Trends-in-Cameroon-in-2024-1.pdf). Through the peg this implies EUR/USD of about 1.048 (655.957 / 625.97) **[derived]**.

**Fuel (administered prices, subsidy cuts)**
- On 3 Feb 2024 pump prices rose about 15%: super from 730 to 840 FCFA/L, gasoil from 720 to 828 FCFA/L. Sources differ slightly (Koaci gives 825 for gasoil). It was the second hike of about 15% in a year, after February 2023. The subsidy fell to 263 bn FCFA — [Ecomatin](https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-le-gouvernement-enterine-une-nouvelle-hausse-des-prix-de-carburant-de-15); [Koaci](https://www.koaci.com/article/2024/02/03/cameroun/economie/cameroun-nouvelle-hausse-du-prix-du-carburant-un-an-apres-la-derniere-augmentation_175565.html); [Journal du Cameroun](https://fr.journalducameroun.com/le-cameroun-va-vers-une-nouvelle-hausse-du-prix-des-carburants-en-2025/); [Cameroon Tribune](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/63038/fr.html/details_2)
- Urban, peri-urban and interurban transport fares were raised in Feb 2024 after the fuel hike — [Koaci 27 Feb 2024](https://www.koaci.com/article/2024/02/27/afrique/politique/cameroun-hausse-du-tarif-de-transport-urbain-periurbain-et-interurbain_176065.html)
- The IMF recommends an **automatic fuel price-setting mechanism** with targeted support (2026 Article IV) — [IMF Mar 2026](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/03/30/pr-26096-cameroon-imf-executive-board-concludes-2026-article-iv-consultation). The IMF estimated that a 1-standard-deviation oil price shock would lift subsidies from about 0.3% to about 1.4% of GDP in 2025 — [IMF CR 2025](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2025/english/1cmrea2025001-print-pdf.pdf.md)

**Taxes / customs (Finance laws)**
- Loi de finances 2026 (Loi n° 2025/012 of 17 Dec 2025):
  - The CEMAC-CEEAC harmonised customs code and common external tariff apply from 1 Jan 2026.
  - **VAT becomes chargeable on advances and deposits (acomptes) for deliveries of goods.**
  - A new environmental tax applies from 1 Jan 2026 to **cement, iron rods, tiles/ceramics** and plastics, payable by local producers and importers (amounts not given in the source).
  — [Deloitte Avocats, LF 2026](https://blog.avocats.deloitte.fr/cameroun-les-principales-innovations-de-la-loi-de-finances-pour-2026/)
- LF 2026 also raises the cost of importing used cars through a new excise duty — [AllAfrica, Mar 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202603020596.html)
- Phones: the customs mechanism comes from LF 2023 (art. 6). The government says it is not a new tax, but media cite **about 33%** duties and taxes on some phones and tablets. Digital customs clearance became mandatory from 1 Apr 2026, and blocking of uncleared handsets was set for 25 May 2026, then postponed to **1 Sep 2026** — [Journal du Cameroun](https://fr.journalducameroun.com/telephones-importes-letat-serre-la-vis-sur-les-droits-de-douane/); [AllAfrica Mar 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202603310614.html); [Burkina24, Aug 2026](https://burkina24.com/2026/08/27/telephonie-mobile-au-cameroun-les-telephones-non-dedouanes-seront-bloques-des-le-1er-septembre-2026/)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** With 2025–26 headline inflation of about 2.5–3.5%, the generic drift over a 12-month lock is about 3%, and about 6–7% over 24 months. Over the 2022–24 shock it was about 6–7% a year, so a 24-month lock signed in early 2022 would have lost about 14% in real terms by early 2024.
- The risk is not mainly headline CPI. It comes from **discrete, policy-driven jumps** (fuel +15% overnight twice in 12 months, phone customs enforcement, environmental tax on cement and iron rods, used-vehicle excise) that a merchant cannot hedge.
- The EUR peg removes FCFA/EUR risk but leaves **USD/CNY risk**. Most phones, motorbikes and appliances come from China or India and are priced in USD/CNY. A 10% EUR/USD fall raises the landed cost of USD-priced goods by about 10% before duties **[derived]**.
- The LF 2026 rule on VAT on advances and deposits for goods matters for MoloPay. If savings are legally treated as deposits on a specific good, VAT may fall due on each instalment, not at handover. This needs tax advice; it affects whether savings are structured as e-money savings or as merchant deposits.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the official INS annual inflation figures for 2020 and 2021 (commonly cited as about 2.5% and 2.3%; unverified here) or the official 2025 annual note.
- No sourced EUR/USD or USD/CNY series for 2020–2026 beyond the December 2024 data point.
- No exact standard VAT rate change found (the 19.25% standard rate, VAT plus surcharge, appears unchanged; not verified in this pass).

## 2. Price trends of key categories (phones, motorbikes, appliances, building materials, rent, school fees)

### Takeaway
Category behaviour diverges:
- **Cement and building materials** rose strongly: about 4,200 to 5,100–5,400 FCFA per 50 kg bag in 2024–25, roughly +25–30%.
- **Rent** in Yaoundé reportedly doubled for mid-range flats in under three years.
- **Smartphones**, unusually, saw rising, not falling, prices in 2026. Transsion's average selling price rose from $77 to $100 in H1 2026 on memory chip costs, and phone customs enforcement added another upward push.
- No reliable sourced data was found for motorbike or appliance price series in Cameroon.

### Cited Findings
**Cement / building materials**
- A 50 kg bag of cement rose from 4,200 to 5,400 FCFA (report of May 2025), alongside steep rises in sand prices — [Le360 Afrique](https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/cameroun-le-prix-des-materiaux-de-construction-demolit-le-reve-de-construire-sa-propre-maison_5CK3GS2ZIJFA7FT5OPKB6HJKSY/)
- The bag price stayed between 5,100 and 5,300 FCFA in Douala and Yaoundé (2025). Producers blame imported clinker and logistics. Clinker imports were 3.107 Mt (97.1 bn FCFA) in 2025, against 2.577 Mt (84.9 bn) in 2024 — [Cameroun24](https://cameroun24.net/article/75671-cameroun-le-geant-chinois-du-ciment-deverse-ses-tonnes-sur-nos-chantiers-le-sac-a-5-000-fcfa-va-t-il-enfin-baisser.html)
- A new entrant (Nono Cement) launched in Yaoundé at 5,000 FCFA per bag, 100–300 FCFA below competitors; a sustained price fall is not yet demonstrated — [Cameroun24](https://cameroun24.net/article/76135-ciment-nono-cement-debarque-a-yaounde-a-5-000-fcfa-le-sac-mais-peut-il-vraiment-casser-les-prix.html)
- The government has repeatedly called cement makers and distributors to order over price surges (Cimencam etc.) — [Ecomatin](https://ecomatin.net/cimenteries-le-gouvernement-rappelle-a-lordre-les-operateurs-suite-a-une-envolee-des-prix-du-ciment). In 2022 the government announced a cement price cut that the market did not follow — [Koaci, Sep 2022](https://www.koaci.com/article/2022/09/08/cameroun/politique/cameroun-le-prix-du-sac-de-ciment-toujours-en-hausse-malgre-la-baisse-annoncee-du-gouvernement_162967.html)
- Ex-factory (industrial producer) prices rose 6.5% year on year in Q1 2025 (INS, via press) — [Ecomatin](https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-avec-une-hausse-de-105-le-secteur-extractif-tire-les-prix-sortie-usine-vers-le-haut)
- From 2026, an environmental tax applies to cement, iron rods and tiles — [Deloitte](https://blog.avocats.deloitte.fr/cameroun-les-principales-innovations-de-la-loi-de-finances-pour-2026/)

**Smartphones (Tecno / Infinix / itel)**
- Transsion's average selling price rose from $77 to $100 in H1 2026, and units fell from 44.5 M to 39.5 M, because AI-driven memory chip costs forced price increases — [China Global South, Oct 2026](https://chinaglobalsouth.com/2026/10/17/transsion-africa-smartphone-market-ai-chip-shortage/)
- Omdia expects Africa's smartphone market to decline in 2026 after a price increase reshaped the market — [Omdia](https://omdia.tech.informa.com/om146997/africas-smartphone-market-is-expected-to-decline-26-in-2026-following-the-first-contraction-in-three-years)
- In 2023, TECNO and Infinix average selling prices rose 26% and 28% year on year (premiumisation) — [Counterpoint, via search summary](https://www.counterpointresearch.com/insights/transsion-quarterly-updates/)
- Low launch prices are explained by local assembly, shared components and mass production of similar models — [TechCabal, Oct 2025](https://techcabal.com/2025/10/20/why-are-infinix-and-tecno-phones-so-cheap-the-real-reasons-explained/)

**Motorbikes**
- Bajaj Cameroon lists Boxer models (e.g. 100 ES) without public prices — [Bajaj CM](https://bajajauto.com/en-cm/bikes/boxer-100es)
- Fuel hikes raised moto-taxi operating costs, and unions called for restraint (2024) — [Cameroon Tribune](https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/62981/fr.html/details_2)

**Rent**
- Yaoundé, Aug 2025: mid-range apartment rents have reportedly **roughly doubled in under three years**, described as a market "flambée" — [AllAfrica/Camer.be, Aug 2025](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202508140330.html)
- Douala has been reported as the most expensive rental city. Prices in Bonabéri rose with an influx of people displaced by the Anglophone crisis (undated, older) — [Camer.be](https://www.camer.be/actualites/economie/cameroun-loyer-dahabitation-la-crise-anglophone-hausse-les-prix-a-douala-cameroon.html)
- Landlords often demand 10–12 months of rent upfront; student housing has been 2–3 times the approved schedule (Yaoundé, 2021) — [Cameroon Tribune](https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/42289/en.html/details_2)
- 2026 indicative ranges: Douala 2-room flats in residential areas (Bonanjo) at 100,000–200,000 FCFA a month; Yaoundé studios in popular districts at 15,000–25,000 FCFA; rents rise around the September–October school term (single guide, not market data) — [infospratiques.cm](https://infospratiques.cm/?p=346)

### Inferences
- **Building materials are the highest-risk category to lock.** There was +25–30% in about 18 months, imported clinker priced in USD, a new environmental tax, and a history of regulatory price interventions that the market ignores. **[ESTIMATE]** Lock of 3–6 months at most, or index-linked.
- **Smartphones**: the usual assumption, that phone prices fall after launch so a lock is safe for the merchant and bad for the customer, **failed in 2026**. Memory cost inflation and customs enforcement pushed prices up. Model obsolescence (see section 3) remains the dominant phone risk over 12–24 months. **[ESTIMATE]** Lock of 6 months at most per specific model.
- **Rent**: landlords already demand 10–12 months upfront, which fits a savings model well, but rents are reset at contract renewal. Locking the rent for an unnamed future unit over 12–24 months is high risk. Lock only a specific identified unit with a landlord commitment, or lock the savings target and not the price.

### Gaps
- No sourced Cameroon retail price series for Bajaj/Haojue/Nanfang motorbikes, fridges or freezers, or model-level Tecno/Infinix price paths after launch. Field price surveys of Douala and Yaoundé dealers would be needed.
- No sourced data on school fees trends 2020–2026.

## 3. Product lifecycle risk (discontinuation during long savings periods)

### Takeaway
The sources found give no Cameroon-specific data on discontinuation. Budget-phone lines are refreshed fast (premiumisation, frequent new models), so a specific SKU locked for 12–24 months will very likely be superseded. MoloPay needs explicit substitution and refund rules.

### Cited Findings
- Transsion's model is built on mass production of similar models with shared components and frequent new launches — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2025/10/20/why-are-infinix-and-tecno-phones-so-cheap-the-real-reasons-explained/)
- Average selling price premiumisation (+26–28% in 2023) and 2026 cost-driven price increases mean the "equivalent" successor model often costs more — [Counterpoint](https://www.counterpointresearch.com/insights/transsion-quarterly-updates/); [China Global South](https://chinaglobalsouth.com/2026/10/17/transsion-africa-smartphone-market-ai-chip-shortage/)
- Customs enforcement from Sep 2026 means uncleared (grey) stock may be blocked, so a merchant substituting with grey-market units creates consumer risk — [Burkina24](https://burkina24.com/2026/08/27/telephonie-mobile-au-cameroun-les-telephones-non-dedouanes-seront-bloques-des-le-1er-septembre-2026/)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** Phone model cycles of about 6–12 months mean a 12–24 month lock on a named SKU will often hit discontinuation. Rules should define "equivalent" by objective specs (RAM/storage/brand tier), set a customer choice between equivalent product, upgrade with the price difference paid, or full refund, and require only customs-cleared devices.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on actual Tecno/Infinix/itel model lifespans in Cameroon, or on how Cameroonian retailers handle substitution.

## 4. Precedents: layaway / price-lock / price protection

### Takeaway
Layaway precedents lock the price, but they work over short horizons (weeks to about 3 months), use small fees, and push price-drop risk onto suppliers (Walmart). South Africa caps lay-by cancellation penalties at 1% of price. Kenyan "Lipa Mdogo Mdogo" schemes show the reputational danger: customers paying 2 times the cash price. No precedent found holds a fixed price for 12–24 months on volatile goods without a fee, index or escape clause.

### Cited Findings
- **Walmart (US)**: layaway items are held in a "layaway liability" status. If the price falls before pickup, the customer pays the new, lower price, and Walmart demanded that **suppliers** fund the difference (example: an $800 TV dropping to $725 means $75 per unit charged back; six-figure chargeback claims in 2016). Advisers told suppliers to write "no price protection on layaway" into contracts — [Talk Business & Politics, Sep 2017](https://talkbusiness.net/2017/09/the-supply-side-wal-mart-seeks-price-protection-on-layaway-items/)
- Walmart's holiday layaway carried a small fee (changed over time, cut after complaints in 2012) and a short window (about Aug/Sep to mid-December) — [Drug Store News](https://drugstorenews.com/news/amid-customer-complaints-walmart-cuts-layaway-fee); [Fortune 2015](https://fortune.com/2015/08/27/walmart-holiday-layaway/)
- Store price-adjustment windows are typically about 7 days; layaway generally falls outside them (secondary blog source) — [sweepstakesbible](https://sweepstakesbible.com/blog/?p=1884)
- **South Africa CPA s.62 (lay-by)**: the consumer may cancel. Payments remain the consumer's money until delivery. The cancellation penalty must be reasonable and **may not exceed 1% of the full purchase price** (CPA Regulations, reg. 34). Secondary sources say no penalty applies on death or hospitalisation — [acts.co.za s.62](https://www.acts.co.za/consumer-protection/62_lay_bys); [acts.co.za reg. 34](https://www.acts.co.za/consumer-protection-act-/r293_34__maximum_amount_of_cancellation_penalty_for_lay_by_s.php); [The Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/business/personal-finance/your-consumer-rights-in-a-lay-by-transaction)
- **Safaricom Lipa Mdogo Mdogo (Kenya)**: this is credit, not savings. The phone is delivered upfront and paid daily, weekly or monthly; T&C amended March 2024 — [Safaricom T&C 2024](https://safaricom.co.ke/images/Downloads/TERMS-AND-CONDITIONS-FOR-THE-SAFARICOM-LIPA-MDOGO-MDOGO-SERVICE-2024.pdf). In wider "lipa pole pole" schemes, a phone with a cash price of KSh 23,000 ended up costing over KSh 50,000, with hidden charges and complaints — [Nation](https://beta.nation.co.ke/kenya/business/exposed-kenya-s-phones-bikes-lipa-pole-pole-nightmare--5225604); [Mwakilishi, Sep 2025](https://www.mwakilishi.com/article/kenya-news/2025-09-03/kenyans-trapped-in-costly-lipa-mdogo-mdogo-credit-schemes); [Business Daily](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/why-buy-now-pay-later-shoppers-face-higher-prices-5112562)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE / design]** A sustainable MoloPay lock needs:
  - (a) category-specific maximum lock durations;
  - (b) an explicit, upfront **price-lock premium** of about the expected drift plus a buffer. With 2025–26 inflation of about 3% this is roughly 2–4% for 6 months on stable categories, rising for volatile ones;
  - (c) a "lower of locked or current price" clause where the merchant passes on price drops, mirroring Walmart; this builds trust and is cheap when prices fall;
  - (d) a free cancellation or refund right with at most a small (≤1%, SA benchmark) fee;
  - (e) a force-majeure or administered-price clause for fuel-type or tax-type shocks, stated upfront.
- Hedging: no FCFA/USD or commodity hedging instruments are accessible to Cameroonian SMEs (I found no source on SME hedging; inference). Practical "hedges" are platform pooling of risk across many locks, the premium, supplier price agreements (Walmart-style supplier protection in reverse), and buying stock early.
- Positioning a **savings** product, where the customer receives the good only when fully paid, avoids the Lipa Mdogo Mdogo credit-cost criticism. Marketing must still avoid implying an unconditional freeze.

### Gaps
- No academic study of layaway price-protection economics found in this pass. No data found on merchant tolerance for price locks in Cameroon; this needs merchant interviews.

## 5. Stock reservation economics

### Takeaway
I found no Cameroon-specific source on inventory carrying costs. Holding reserved stock for 12–24 months ties up merchant working capital, risks obsolescence (phones) and spoilage or quality loss (cement has a limited shelf life). This argues for reserving stock only at a savings threshold, or at completion, with "available on order" lead times.

### Cited Findings
- 2024 inflation by origin: imported goods +4.0%, local +4.7% — [INS](https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Annual-Report-on-Price-Trends-in-Cameroon-in-2024-1.pdf). This gives a reference point for the yearly cost of *not* holding stock, compared with holding it.
- Walmart moves layaway stock out of saleable inventory into a "layaway liability" status — [Talk Business](https://talkbusiness.net/2017/09/the-supply-side-wal-mart-seeks-price-protection-on-layaway-items/)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** Merchant cost of holding a 300,000 FCFA phone for 12 months = cost of capital (Cameroonian SME lending is often in the mid-teens percent; unsourced here) + obsolescence (phone value can fall significantly over 12 months) + storage. This easily exceeds 15–25% of value, far above the price drift the lock protects against (about 3–5% a year in 2025–26). So **physical reservation at agreement is economically irrational for long locks**. Reserve at a threshold (e.g. 50–80% saved), or at 100% with a stated delivery lead time.
- For cement, physical holding beyond a few months is also a quality risk (common industry guidance; not sourced here). Lock the price per bag, not specific bags.

### Gaps
- No sourced figures on Cameroonian SME working-capital interest rates or inventory carrying costs in this pass (BEAC/COBAC lending rate data would close this).

## 6. Consumer protection angle (Cameroon)

### Takeaway
The Cameroon framework law n° 2011/012 of 6 May 2011 on consumer protection bans erroneous, misleading ("mensongère") or abusive advertising and requires advertising to comply with price-display and terms-of-sale rules. A "frozen price" claim therefore has to state all conditions (duration, fees, substitution, exceptions) upfront.

### Cited Findings
- Loi-cadre 2011/012 sets the general framework for consumer protection and strictly prohibits erroneous, misleading or abusive advertising. Art. 14: any advertising to consumers must comply with the rules on price advertising and terms of sale — [Loi 2011/012 (ART PDF)](https://www.art.cm/sites/default/files/documents/loi_cadre_2011_012_06052011_protect_conso.pdf); [search summary](https://leap.unep.org/en/countries/cm/national-legislation/loi-cadre-ndeg-2011012-du-6-mai-2011-portant-protection-du)
- Academic commentary: "mensongère" implies intent to deceive, as distinct from erroneous or abusive advertising; the law does not expressly target "publicité trompeuse" — [Memoire Online](https://www.memoireonline.com/09/25/14733/La-protection-du-consommateur-contre-la-publicite-illegale.html)
- Related laws: law n° 2006/018 on advertising; law n° 2015/018 on commercial activity — [Memoire Online](https://www.memoireonline.com/09/25/14733/La-protection-du-consommateur-contre-la-publicite-illegale.html)
- Adhesion contract (standard terms) issues under 2011/012 are discussed in academic work — [Memoire Online](https://www.memoireonline.com/04/15/9081/m_Les-contrats-d-adhesion-a-l-epreuve-de-la-loi-cadre-n2011012-du-06-mai-2011-portant-protection-d46.html)

### Inferences
- Use wording such as "prix garanti X mois" with conditions, not an unconditional "prix figé". Publish per category: lock duration, premium, substitution rule, refund right and timeline, and exceptions (administered price or tax changes, if any). Unilateral price revisions not stated upfront risk being treated as abusive or misleading.
- Check how the LF 2026 rule on VAT on advances and deposits interacts with how the savings are characterised (see section 1).

### Gaps
- I did not read the full text of 2011/012 articles on abusive clauses or sanctions, or any implementing decrees; legal review is needed.
